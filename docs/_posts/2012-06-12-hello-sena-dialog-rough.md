I
sat down with my friend Tomoko and did an initial reading of my script [Hello
Seña](/2011/11/03/hello-sena-the-script/).

It’s been a while since I did anything related to [Little
Robots](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629070841/http://rl337.org/category/littlerobots/?sortby=ID&order=ASC),
so this was fun.  Naturally, this is not the final recording.  My Japanese is pretty obnoxious, but
it’ll help a lot for [storyboarding](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storyboarding) the scene.

Here’s a spliced together dialog.  You can hear a lot of background noise in this… I’ll have to
figure out how to clean it up.  The recording itself was done on my
[iPhone4S](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iphone4s).  Tomoko and I used
[Skype](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skype) for the actual conversation.
