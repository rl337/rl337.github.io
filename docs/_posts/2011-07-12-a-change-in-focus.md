So I gave Drupal a try but found that I had no patience for actually writing HTML or messing with
the hundreds of little plugins… blah. How things have changed… so.. hello WordPress!

A couple weeks ago, I had decided to enter the [Exile Villify video
contest](http://www.thinkwithportals.com/videocontest/). I started
down the road of modeling out the various elements that I wanted in the video … but a combination of
lazyness and [Rock Band 3](http://www.rockband.com/games/rb3) got in the way.

[Archived image: A Turret and
Boxes][archive-link-1]I
did learn an awful lot about the [subdivision
surfaces](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivision_surfaces) in
[blender](http://www.blender.org), though… so I can’t say that it was a complete waste of
time…  I found that the objects in this scene, though they look primitive, are actually pretty
complex… The turret in particular, with its rounded shape yet sharp edges.  I definitely had to get
competent in cinching edges and marking seems.

Since acknowledging that I won’t make the deadline for the music video, I’ve decided to morph the
entry in my [In-Flight
Projects](https://web.archive.org/web/20111020171911/http://rl337.org/projects-in-flight/)
page to a more general non-Aperture Science branded set of robots making music.  It’ll be a good
excuse for me to mess with the [Structured Audio](http://www.cs.berkeley.edu/~lazzaro/sa/)
bits of MPEG4.
