[Archived image: The Lady of Thorn Keep, Part
1][archive-link-1]One
of my on-going writing projects is a collaboration with artist and friend
[Step](http://lady-cybercat.deviantart.com/)[hanie Stone](http://lady-cybercat.deviantart.com/).
Together we’re bringing together the world of Thorn Keep, a fantasy setting where clans of
anthropomorphic humans suffer through nominally fantasy setting kinds of things… war, politics,
betrayal, etc.

The overall world was Steph’s invention.  She came to me with an initial plot and a few key
characters.  It was my job to flesh things out and breath some life into the skeletal beginnings of
the concept.  Once I’ve written and edited a section of story, Steph will then produce an artistic
rendering from that segment.  The image you see to the right is the first such example.  You can
keep up with the story either through the artist’s [Deviant
Art](http://lady-cybercat.deviantart.com/) account, or through my pages here.   The original piece
produced is then auctioned off over at [FurBuy](http://www.furbuy.com).

I will also keep a running storyboard here as a
[page](https://web.archive.org/web/20110930044411/http://rl337.org/projects-in-flight/the-lady-of-thorn-keep/)
off the blog.  We’ve just put out our second installment… Stay tuned for more updates.
