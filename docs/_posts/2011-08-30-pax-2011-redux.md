[![floor](/assets/images/legacy-blog/2011/08/floor-300x224.jpg.webp)](/assets/images/legacy-blog/2011/08/floor.jpg.webp)

This
year, I was fortunate enough to inherit a 3-day pass to [PAX Prime](http://prime.paxsite.com/) 2011,
courtesy of my long time friend and compatriot, [Nick Hernandez](https://twitter.com/#!/Nrrrdking).

Even though I’ve seen the various PAX episodes on [PAtv](http://www.penny-arcade.com/patv/), but
nothing prepared me for the sheer magnitude of the expo.

[![prototype-2](/assets/images/legacy-blog/2011/08/prototype-2-300x224.jpg.webp)](/assets/images/legacy-blog/2011/08/prototype-2.jpg.webp)
To give you an example of what I’m talking about, here is a picture of the demo area for the game
[Prototype 2](http://www.prototypegame.com/).  You can see, they didn’t just put up a projector to
demo stuff… they built an entire helicopter prop with sounds… and this was on the tame side as far
as booths go.

[![warthog](/assets/images/legacy-blog/2011/08/warthog-300x224.jpg.webp)](/assets/images/legacy-blog/2011/08/warthog.jpg.webp)

Probably
the most extreme example of commercial promotion of a francise was [Halo
Fest](http://halo.xbox.com/en-us/news/blog/HaloFest/49437).  Basically, it was a mini convention ….
within a convention!  There were complex dioramas.. scale vehicles like this
[Warthog](http://halo.wikia.com/wiki/M12_Light_Reconnaissance_Vehicle).. and TONS of space dedicated
do game play.

[![cartridges](/assets/images/legacy-blog/2011/08/cartridges-300x224.jpg.webp)](/assets/images/legacy-blog/2011/08/cartridges.jpg.webp)

This
isn’t to say that PAX is not just some orgy of AAA title marketing… there are a LOT of
for-the-community things… like the [Classic Console
Freeplay](http://forums.penny-arcade.com/discussion/124229/classic-console-freeplay/p1).  Ever want
to play an [original NES](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_Entertainment_System) game but
couldn’t afford it as a kid?  This is the place for you.

All in all, my PAX experience was awesome.  I am totally going back next year. Period.
