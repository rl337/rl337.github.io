Today would be [Bob Ross](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Ross)‘s 70th
birthday. [![Bob_at_Easel](/assets/images/legacy-blog/2012/10/Bob_at_Easel-232x300.jpeg.webp)](/assets/images/legacy-blog/2012/10/Bob_at_Easel.jpeg.webp)

For those of you who aren’t familiar with Bob Ross, he was an artist who’s best known for his PBS
show, [The Joy of Painting](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Joy_of_Painting).  I’d say that Bob
Ross informed a LOT of my creative processes growing up. I’d watch this show religiously even though
my family would never have been able to afford oil paints or canvas.   For a short while, I tried to
reproduce his techniques with poster paint and notebook paper, but without much success.

According to the [wikipedia](http://wikipedia.org) article, Bob spent time in the [Air
Force](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_force) before dedicating himself to painting.  It’s hard to
imagine the painter in a military environment… but apparently, he got quite a lot of the
inspirations for his famous landscapes from where he was stationed in
[Alaska](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eielson_Air_Force_Base).

Happy Birthday, Bob.  Thank you for being such an inspiration.
